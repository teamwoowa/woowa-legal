Local play and your choices

You can play local content without creating a login account or giving us an email address. The app keeps settings, calibration, progress, local scores, initials/nickname, a coarse age band, service choices, bounded diagnostics, pending requests and access-state information on your device. It does not request your date of birth. Optional advertising is unavailable for unknown ages and under-16 users in this implementation; there is no guardian-verification exception.

App analytics and rank-change push notifications are disabled in this release. Advertising choices do not enable them. This does not mean that the operating system, Google Play, advertising/consent services or hosting providers process no technical information.

Local data remains until changed or removed, subject to device backup/restore behavior. Clearing local storage does not delete server/provider records and may remove installation credentials needed to identify related records. The current diagnostic store is capacity-limited to 20 matching files, 20 MiB overall and 4 MiB per session, rather than a fixed number of days. Older diagnostic locations require separate cleanup.

Optional public leaderboard

The current Expert leaderboard receives board identifiers, your client-calculated score, chosen initials and a random submission identifier. The public board displays initials/name, score and a derived position, with up to ten displayed rows per board. Do not use real names, contact information or other private details in your initials.

Local play does not itself submit a public score. Qualification/board reads and deliberate registration are separate operations. The current submission path does not send input replay for server rescoring or a private record-ownership credential in that payload. A public nickname or submission identifier does not prove private ownership. A ten-row display is not a promise that all related submissions, logs or backups contain only ten records or are automatically erased.

The current route does not provide a verified installation-authenticated self-service export/deletion operation for every public record. Contact us for access or removal requests. We may need proportionate information to locate a record and establish authority, and may be unable to safely link a record using a public identifier alone. Connection providers also process IP/network information when delivering or securing requests.

Optional advertising and privacy choices

The initial advertising test uses Google's official demonstration rewarded-ad unit. A demo ad does not grant game access. Advertising is requested explicitly outside song playback. A separate owned-unit reward test would require recognized SDK test-device configuration and server verification; it is not activated merely by publishing this notice or a consent message.

Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process IP/connection information, approximate location derived from IP, device/app or advertising identifiers, consent information, ad interactions and diagnostics, depending on the request, SDK configuration and choices. Consent-information and privacy-option requests may contact Google even when an ad cannot be loaded.

The applicable Google-managed message identifies configured advertising purposes and partners and offers the available consent and legitimate-interest choices. Review that message and its partner list; no third-party SDK is described as active simply because an adapter exists in the project. The existing account-wide partner configuration is not changed by approval of this notice. Google has independent-controller responsibilities for certain AdMob processing and acts as processor for some specific features; it is not solely our processor for everything.

You can turn off the app's optional advertising choice in Service settings and revisit advertising privacy options when available. A missing or failed form is not consent and is not bypassed. Rejecting consent does not mean that all necessary security or connection processing stops. Ordinary local play does not require advertising consent, a login or public score registration.

If a separately approved verified-reward test is later used, its verification path may additionally process a pseudonymous guest/attempt binding, request/receipt identifiers, verification status and access state to prevent duplicate grants. Demo completion alone is never that verification.

Google information: AdMob processing roles, Google privacy policy, partner-site/app processing, EU user-consent policy.

Google Play technical purchases

This build uses an isolated google-license-test commerce environment, not an approved live-sales profile. Internal-track membership and an in-app test flag do not guarantee a free purchase. Testers must use the configured Play license-test account and a clearly identified Google test payment method. Real charged payments are not authorized.

Google Play handles payment-method information under its applicable terms. This integration does not receive full payment-card details. The app/backend processes product/option identifiers, purchase tokens and hashes, a pseudonymous/obfuscated guest binding, purchase/acknowledgement/refund observations, verification timestamps, rights, pending/recovery state and restore challenges for requested verification and access. The implementation includes encrypted token recovery and Android encrypted credential/state storage; encryption is not an absolute security guarantee. These records are not app analytics.

Restoration requires a fresh Google-owned-purchase query and server verification, not a pasted token or cached screen. The intended association is non-exclusive across legitimate installations, not cross-store account linking. Actual transaction, acknowledgement, pending/cancellation and multi-device restoration acceptance are separate tests and are not established by this notice.

The temporary host has a seven-day technical lifecycle and seven-day encrypted-token TTL. Expiry is not a promise that every guest, rights, recovery, security or backup record is physically deleted on day seven. The operator reviews test records at test closure and removes records once no longer needed for test resolution, valid rights, recovery or proportionate security/replay protection. Later live commerce requires a separate verified operating profile and updated notice; valid purchase recovery may require encrypted tokens for years, not this temporary test TTL. No universal statutory transaction-retention period is asserted.

Google information: Play terms, Google Payments privacy.
